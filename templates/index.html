    Hemodynamic Biomarkers Explained

    Understanding the biological mechanisms captured by our feature engineering pipeline.

    Pulse Pressure ($PP$)

    Computed as $ap\_hi - ap\_lo$. Reflects vascular compliance and arterial stiffness. Values above 50 mmHg correlate with accelerated coronary artery damage.

    Mean Arterial Pressure ($MAP$)

    Estimated as $ap\_lo + \frac{1}{3}PP$. Represents average arterial pressure during a single cardiac cycle, serving as a primary indicator of organ tissue perfusion.

    AHA Blood Pressure Stages

    Classified into Normal (<120/80), Elevated (120-129/<80), Stage 1 (130-139/80-89), Stage 2 (≥140/≥90), and Hypertensive Crisis (>180/>120).
